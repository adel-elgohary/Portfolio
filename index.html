01 — About

Grounded in networks, moving into automated infrastructure.

I'm a Computers & Information Technology student at Menofia University, building my path into cloud and DevOps engineering one system at a time.

My starting point wasn't the cloud — it was networking. Routing and switching, VLAN segmentation, firewalls, IPsec/SSL VPNs. That background shapes how I think about infrastructure now: I care about what happens at the packet level as much as what happens inside a container.

From there I moved into automation and cloud: Docker and Kubernetes for packaging and orchestration, Terraform and Ansible for infrastructure as code, Jenkins for CI/CD, and AWS for the platform underneath it all — with Prometheus for keeping an eye on whether any of it is actually working.

I'm currently completing structured, hands-on training through DEPI and NTI, and looking for a role — internship, graduate program, or junior position — where I can keep building real infrastructure, not just lab exercises.

04 — Featured Project

Enterprise Network Infrastructure

A segmented, routed, and secured enterprise network — designed and validated end to end in Cisco Packet Tracer.

08/2025

Enterprise Network Infrastructure

Cisco Packet Tracer · VLANs · Static Routing · Inter-VLAN Routing · Port Security
PROBLEM

A single flat network doesn't scale for a growing organization — no segmentation between departments, no controlled routing, and no protection against unauthorized device access.

ARCHITECTURE

Three routers connected end-to-end, with department-level segmentation via VLANs, trunk links carrying inter-VLAN traffic, and a full IP addressing & subnetting scheme across the topology.

ENGINEERING
  • Configured VLANs, trunk links, and inter-VLAN routing
  • Set up static routing across all three routers
  • Applied port security per switch port
  • Designed the IP addressing & subnetting scheme
RESULT

Verified end-to-end connectivity across all VLANs and routers, with segmented broadcast domains and secured access at the switch port level. Tested and troubleshot until the topology held under normal traffic conditions.
