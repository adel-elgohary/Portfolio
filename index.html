01 — About

Grounded in networks, moving into automated infrastructure.

I'm a Computers & Information Technology student at Menofia University, building my path into cloud and DevOps engineering one system at a time.

My starting point wasn't the cloud — it was networking. Routing and switching, VLAN segmentation, firewalls, IPsec/SSL VPNs. That background shapes how I think about infrastructure now: I care about what happens at the packet level as much as what happens inside a container.

From there I moved into automation and cloud: Docker and Kubernetes for packaging and orchestration, Terraform and Ansible for infrastructure as code, Jenkins for CI/CD, and AWS for the platform underneath it all — with Prometheus for keeping an eye on whether any of it is actually working.

I'm currently completing structured, hands-on training through DEPI and NTI, and looking for a role — internship, graduate program, or junior position — where I can keep building real infrastructure, not just lab exercises.
